## Provenance — what the files themselves say (verified, not assumed)

1.mp4 and 2.mp4 embed **C2PA content credentials** in their `udta/meta/ilst`
metadata. Reading those manifests yields:

    c2pa.created  when = 2026-09-26T09:06:22Z
      softwareAgent: name = "Volcengine_Ark_CN", version = "1.0.0"
      model_name   = "doubao-seedance-2-5"
      digitalSourceType = http://cv.iptc.org/newscodes/digitalsourcetype/trainedAlgorithmicMedia

    c2pa.created  when = 2026-09-26T04:04:17Z
      softwareAgent: name = "Volcengine_Ark_CN", version = "1.0.0"
      model_name   = "doubao-seedance-2-0"
      digitalSourceType = http://cv.iptc.org/newscodes/digitalsourcetype/trainedAlgorithmicMedia

i.e. **these are AI-generated clips** (Doubao/Seedance video models served by
Volcengine Ark). 3.mp4 carries an x264 encoder tag but no C2PA manifest.

The upstream project describes the clips as the author's own animation work. That
description is **inaccurate as far as the metadata shows** — we redistribute them
under the MIT license they came with, keep this notice, and record the discrepancy
here rather than repeating the claim.

## What this means if you reuse them

- The MIT grant above covers what the upstream project distributed; we pass it on
  unchanged.
- Separately, AI-generated media may also be subject to the terms of the service
  that produced it (here: Volcengine Ark / Doubao). **We have not reviewed those
  terms.** If you plan to publish or commercialise these clips, check them yourself
  — or simply replace the clips with your own (see below).
- No personal information was found in the clips: a printable-string scan over all
  three files found no user names, home directories or drive paths. The only
  identifying strings are the service/model names and timestamps quoted above.

If you do not want third-party media in your copy, delete this directory (or point
the `dir` setting somewhere else) — boot-splash falls back to a plain gradient.
